02b · Regulatory mapping

NIST AI RMF crosswalk

Federal and enterprise procurement teams operating under OMB Memorandum M-24-10 can use this crosswalk to map every Suite spec — and the open-source implementation stack — to specific NIST AI RMF subcategories. The AI Procurement Decision Card (spec #11) is the natural carrier for RMF-aligned procurement outcomes — its criteria.rubric field can record per-subcategory pass / partial / fail results for any vendor review.
