provenance-lab.kineticgain.com

Watermark Robustness Lab

An educational, in-browser lab that injects a synthetic, SynthID-style watermark and lets you attack it (notch filtering, denoising, and more) to show why hidden in-band watermarks are fragile, and why durable provenance needs signed C2PA Content Credentials instead. It does not detect, contain, or remove Google SynthID or any production watermark.
