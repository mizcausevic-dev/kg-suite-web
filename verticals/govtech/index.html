GovTech / Public Sector AI vertical 6-pack

GovTech / Public Sector AI
6 sibling specs · 1 canonical shape per artifact

Six sibling specs for AI tools used BY government — federal agency benefit determinations, federal contracting recommendations, FOIA processing, IRS / SSA / VA / DHS / state-Medicaid decision support, public-services chatbots, regulatory permitting. Distinct from the prior 6 verticals (which cover regulators telling the private sector what to do): GovTech covers government's OWN AI use rules. These draft profiles preserve mappings to 2024 OMB memoranda. They have not been remapped to the replacement guidance and must not be used to assert current federal AI readiness or required reporting.

Reference sources and status

OMB M-25-21 rescinded and replaced M-24-10; OMB M-25-22 rescinded and replaced M-24-18 on April 3, 2025. The linked profiles still encode the 2024 memoranda and are historical drafts, not current-guidance conformance artifacts. Other cited sources include Section 508, the Privacy Act, FOIA, NIST AI RMF, FedRAMP, and state AI policies; their applicability was not reassessed in this page correction.

Key design innovation vs sibling-vertical equivalents

FIRST Suite audit stream with THREE orthogonal invariants (human-agency-officer + Federal AI Use Case Inventory entry + classification-clearance via ordered UNCLASSIFIED<CUI<CONFIDENTIAL<SECRET<TOP-SECRET enforcement per E.O. 13526); 'rescinded' lifecycle state in state tracker; Title VI LEP dimension in bias lab; MOST EXTENSIVE Incident Card event-type taxonomy (18 events) including the GovTech-unique federal-ai-use-case-inventory-correction referral pathway; profile-level ai_use_case_inventory_block in the draft Decision Card (mapped to historical OMB M-24-10 §3(a); current obligations need fresh review).

Why parallel structure matters

A buyer's procurement team operating across mixed regulated verticals — HealthTech AI vendors plus FinTech AI vendors plus HR Tech AI vendors — can apply the same six-shape Suite vocabulary to every vendor in every vertical. The kg-suite-vertical-router tool routes any artifact to the right vertical's verification logic with one CLI command. The kg-suite-vertical-comparator tool surfaces the SAME-vs-DIFFERENT design contributions across all eleven verticals as a single reference table.
